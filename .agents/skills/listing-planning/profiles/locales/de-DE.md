# de-DE locale profile

## Language

- German for Germany
- Noun capitalization and German punctuation
- Native German review required for consumer-facing text

## Formatting

- Decimal comma and period thousands separator
- Currency display should follow the channel and legal context
- Date format commonly uses day, month, and year order
- Use metric units unless a verified requirement says otherwise

## Copy QA

- Plan for compound words and text expansion
- Keep terminology consistent across modules and specifications
- Avoid translating claims without preserving their conditions
- Research search language for the selected category and channel

This profile does not define what buyers value, which features matter, or which scenarios to use.
