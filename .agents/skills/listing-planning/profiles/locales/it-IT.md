# it-IT locale profile

## Language

- Italian for Italy
- Italian punctuation, articles, contractions, and sentence flow
- Native Italian review required for consumer-facing text

## Formatting

- Decimal comma and period thousands separator
- Currency display should follow the channel and legal context
- Date format commonly uses day, month, and year order
- Use metric units unless a verified requirement says otherwise

## Copy QA

- Avoid literal source-language syntax
- Check agreement, articles, and terminology consistency
- Preserve claim conditions during localization
- Research search language for the selected category and channel

This profile does not define consumer needs, lifestyle scenes, or product priorities.
