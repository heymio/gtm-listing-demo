# Direct-to-consumer product page profile

## Use when

The brand controls its own product page or landing page.

## Verify for every project

- CMS and component library
- supported media and interactions
- analytics and experiment requirements
- checkout and variant ownership
- accessibility and performance constraints
- responsive breakpoints

A DTC page may use a longer narrative than a marketplace, but every section still needs a distinct role and evidence object. Do not copy marketplace module names into a DTC architecture.
