# Market research reference

## Research equation

```text
Product / Category
× Market
× Locale
× Channel
× Current project evidence
```

Never start from a national stereotype or a permanent category assumption stored in the core workflow.

## Evidence types

Use and label:

1. official government, regulator, standards, or platform sources
2. current channel documentation and account capabilities
3. industry or retailer data
4. search and keyword evidence
5. competitor pages and advertising
6. verified reviews, VOC, support data, and user research
7. social or community signals

Separate evidence from inference. Record source date and scope.

## Region versus country

A region overlay may define shared verification obligations. It cannot replace country-market research, locale-specific copy, or channel-site analysis.

## Dynamic information

Reverify information that can change, including platform modules, prices, laws, certification, availability, subscriptions, product versions, competitor pages, and search behavior.

## Research outputs

Create a Market Evidence Registry containing:

- need state or language
- market
- locale
- channel
- category
- source
- evidence type
- confidence
- permitted use

Unsupported assumptions remain assumptions and cannot enter consumer copy.

## Query design

Construct searches from the project configuration rather than a preset country list. Include the category, use case, target locale, channel, and the specific decision being investigated.

## Claim boundary

External market research may add terminology, scenarios, objections, and purchase motivation. It cannot prove a product capability.
