# en-US locale profile

## Language

- United States English
- US spelling and punctuation
- Native review required for formal consumer copy

## Formatting

- Decimal point and comma thousands separator
- Currency format should match the channel
- Date format should match channel and legal context
- Evaluate imperial and metric units according to product, channel, and applicable requirements

## Copy QA

- Keep title capitalization consistent with channel rules
- Avoid unsupported superlatives and vague benefit language
- Check mobile line length and button labels
- Research search language for the selected category and channel

This profile does not define consumer needs or product priorities.
