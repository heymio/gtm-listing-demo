# Visual production

## Product identity

The referenced real product source is authoritative for product identity. Preserve geometry, ports, controls, accessories, packaging, UI, color, material, and proportions when the role requires exact evidence.

AI may generate approved environment, lighting, atmosphere, or explanatory layers around source-faithful product evidence. Do not invent product structure or functional proof.

## Evidence Mode

`SOURCE_FAITHFUL` requires faithful identity. `CREATIVE_MOCK` may generate environment/context while keeping the real product faithful and treating generated details as non-proof. `PROOF_VISUAL` requires authoritative factual proof sources.

Keep identity source requirements separate from proof source requirements.

## Page Visual System context

Use only the current asset direction plus nearest same-page-region neighbor summaries to preserve coherence without visual convergence.

## Retry

For the same asset and same identified defect, allow at most two autonomous attempts without new user input/evidence.
